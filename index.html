About

I'm a taught-postgraduate student at the University of Hong Kong, pursuing an MSc in Industrial Engineering and Logistics Management (BSc in Decision Analytics, minor in Finance).

I focus on the demand-and-supply planning problems retailers and FMCG supply chains face every day: forecasting demand, setting replenishment and inventory policy, and protecting service levels.

For my MSc dissertation, I'm studying a problem at the heart of demand planning: retailers observe censored sales, not true demand — stockouts distort the demand signal and bias replenishment decisions. I'm working on detecting, quantifying, and correcting that bias while balancing expected profit against downside risk.

Previously I worked as a Research Assistant in predictive analytics at HKU and as a Data Analyst Intern at CITIC Securities — building and evaluating predictive models (including LSTM time-series forecasting), cleaning large datasets, and communicating findings to non-technical audiences.
